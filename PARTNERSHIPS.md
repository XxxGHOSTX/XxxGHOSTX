## Overview

Thalos Prime is designed for commercial deployment across multiple verticals and
use cases. Strategic partnerships are a core element of the go-to-market strategy,
enabling Thalos Prime to reach enterprise customers, regulated industries, and
global markets through trusted intermediary relationships.

This document describes the partnership models, eligibility criteria, and
representative commercial frameworks that the owner intends to offer.

---

## Partnership Categories

### 1. Technology Integration Partners

**Description:** Technology companies, SaaS vendors, or platform providers that
integrate Thalos Prime capabilities into their products or services to enhance
their own value proposition.

**Examples of fit:**
- DevOps and infrastructure management platforms.
- AI-assisted software development tools.
- Enterprise knowledge management systems.
- Compliance and audit tooling for regulated industries.

**Proposed Terms:**
- License Thalos Prime components under an OEM or technology licensing agreement.
- Revenue share or royalty on licenses where Thalos Prime is embedded: **typically
  10–25% of incremental revenue attributable to Thalos Prime** (exact rate negotiated
  per agreement).
- Co-development opportunities available where the integration benefits the core
  ecosystem (subject to IP Policy).

---

### 2. System Integrator (SI) Partners

**Description:** Consulting firms, managed service providers, and system integrators
that deploy Thalos Prime for enterprise end customers as part of larger transformation
or technology projects.

**Examples of fit:**
- Digital transformation consultancies.
- Government IT contractors.
- Industry-specific technology service providers (healthcare IT, financial services
  technology, etc.).

**Proposed Terms:**
- Authorized Reseller or Deployment Partner license.
- **Reseller margin: 15–30% of referred/deployed customer contract value** (exact
  rate negotiated per agreement).
- SI partners receive access to technical enablement resources, implementation guides,
  and priority support for partner-led deployments.
- SLA pass-through agreements available for enterprise end customers.

---

### 3. Referral Partners

**Description:** Individuals or organizations that refer prospective customers to
Thalos Prime. No technical integration required.

**Proposed Terms:**
- **Referral fee: 10–15% of the first-year contract value** for successfully
  closed referred customers (exact rate negotiated per agreement).
- Referral credited upon customer contract execution and initial payment.
- Referral agreement required; informal referrals do not create an obligation.

---

### 4. Academic and Research Partners

**Description:** Universities, research institutions, and academic departments
interested in using Thalos Prime for research purposes, with potential for
co-publication and knowledge transfer.

**Proposed Terms:**
- Research licensing at reduced or waived commercial fees, subject to:
  - Non-commercial use restriction.
  - Acknowledgement of Thalos Prime in any publications.
  - Sharing of research findings with the owner where applicable.
  - Compliance with the CLA for any code contributions arising from the research.
- Co-authorship or technical collaboration on publications may be offered at the
  owner's discretion.

---

### 5. Strategic Investors and Co-Development Partners

**Description:** Organizations that wish to invest in or co-develop the Thalos Prime
ecosystem in exchange for equity, preferred commercial terms, or exclusive access
to capabilities.

**Proposed Terms:**
- Negotiated individually.
- May include preferred pricing, early access, co-development rights, and board or
  advisory participation.
- Subject to formal investment or co-development agreement reviewed by qualified counsel.

*See [INVESTOR_OVERVIEW.md](INVESTOR_OVERVIEW.md) for the investment opportunity overview.*

## General Partnership Terms

The following general terms apply to all partnership categories unless superseded
by a specific signed agreement:

### Eligibility

Partners must:
1. Operate a legitimate, registered business (where applicable) or be a duly
   authorized academic institution.
2. Comply with all applicable laws, including export control regulations.
3. Execute a signed Partnership Agreement before any partnership rights take effect.
4. Maintain confidentiality of proprietary information shared in the partnership context.

### Intellectual Property

- Thalos Prime intellectual property (including source code, algorithms, and brand
  assets) remains the exclusive property of Tony Ray Macier III at all times.
- Partners do not acquire any ownership interest in Thalos Prime IP through the
  partnership.
- Any jointly developed technology will be subject to specific IP terms in the
  partnership agreement.
- See [IP_POLICY.md](IP_POLICY.md) for the full IP policy.

### Confidentiality

Partners with access to proprietary materials (source code, unreleased features,
pricing, business plans) must maintain strict confidentiality and may not disclose
such information to third parties without written authorization.

### SLA Framework (Proposed for SI and Technology Partners)

| Tier | Response Time Target | Uptime Target |
|---|---|---|
| Critical (production down) | 4 hours (business hours) | N/A |
| High (degraded functionality) | 8 business hours | N/A |

*SLA terms are a planning framework. Final SLAs are negotiated and stated in the

### Revenue Share Structures (Proposed)

| Partner Type | Base Revenue Share (Proposed) |
|---|---|
| Technology / OEM partner | 10–25% of incremental revenue |
| System integrator | 15–30% of referred contract value |
| Referral partner | 10–15% of first-year contract value |
| Strategic partner | Individually negotiated |

*All revenue share percentages are planning assumptions. Final terms are negotiated
and stated in signed agreements. See [ASSUMPTIONS.md](ASSUMPTIONS.md).*
