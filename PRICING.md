## Overview

Thalos Prime is offered under a proprietary license. Access to any component of
the ecosystem — including source code, APIs, SDKs, and services — requires a
formal licensing or subscription agreement with Tony Ray Macier III.

The following tiers are proposed to serve different customer profiles, from
individual developers to large enterprise organizations.

## Pricing Principles

1. **Transparency**: Pricing will be clearly documented in formal agreements.
2. **Fairness**: Smaller teams pay less; larger deployments and more value pay more.
3. **Alignment**: Pricing is designed to align with the value delivered, not with
   artificial feature restrictions.
4. **Negotiability**: Enterprise and strategic pricing is always negotiable.
